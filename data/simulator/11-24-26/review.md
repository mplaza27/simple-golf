## Distance diagnosis
- **Speed is good.** With likely misreads removed, your 7-iron averaged 78 mph club speed. That's about a 10–11 handicap's speed, and faster than LPGA players (76 mph), who carry it 141 yd.
- **Contact is where the yards go.** Your 7-iron ball speed was 97 mph (LPGA 104) for a 1.24 smash, about what a 27 handicap gets. Within each club, carry tracks smash closely (r = 0.72). Solid contact at the speed you already have is worth about +11 yd per iron (7i +6, 8i +8, 9i +16, PW +15).
- **You're adding loft at impact.** 22° launch with a 27.5° 7-iron and 23° with a 31.5° 8-iron; Tour players launch at about half their loft. Extra loft turns speed into height instead of ball speed. - **Contact may slip on faster swings.** Comparing each club only with itself, faster swings had somewhat lower smash, mostly on the 8-iron and 9-iron (r ≈ −0.36 over 25 shots). With 5–6 shots per club this is a hint, not proof.
