## Heel strikes with the long clubs
- **What you noticed:** warm-up drives and woods came off the heel or hosel until you aimed to strike about a clubhead closer to you; then contact improved.
- **The data agrees on contact:** second half vs first half of each block: driver smash 1.29 → 1.38 (+5 mph ball speed), 3-hybrid +10 mph ball speed and +13 yd carry, 7-wood +9 mph and +13 yd.
- **But not on direction:** later drives finished further right (39 → 72 yd) with twice the side spin. Centred contact fixed the strike; the curve comes from the face.
- **Driver needs a face fix, irons need a path fix:** driver path is nearly neutral (−1.7°) but the face is +3.7° open to the target, so drives start right and curve right. Irons are the other way round: face near square, path −6 to −9° out-to-in.
- **Why the heel:** reaching toward the ball (standing up or moving closer through the swing, the early extension that S-posture encourages) and an out-to-in move both push the hosel into the ball. "Aim a head closer" is a good compensation; the setup and wall drills are the long-term fix.
