## Diagnosis
- **The path is the main problem, not the face.** Full swings (7i–AW, 30 shots) averaged −9.2° path, with the face only −1.7° to target. That leaves the face +7.5° open to the path: a pull-slice that finished 9–18 yd right on 7i–9i. 28 of 30 swings were −6° or worse.
- **Strike is below where it should be, even for a 25 handicap.** Median iron smash was 1.21. Tour irons run 1.28–1.38, and amateur irons are commonly quoted around 1.25–1.30 (a rule of thumb, not a measured 25-handicap average). Low smash usually means heel or thin contact. Note this is inferred: the export has no face-contact location, so check the swing frames in the shot viewer to confirm heel strikes.
- **Contact may slip on faster swings.** Comparing each club only with itself, faster swings had somewhat lower smash, mostly on the 8-iron and 9-iron (r ≈ −0.36 over 25 shots, after removing likely misreads). With 5–6 shots per club this is a hint, not proof.
- **Your speed is near LPGA level; your strike isn't.** Your 7-iron club speed averaged 74 mph (LPGA ~76) for 127 yd carry, versus 141 yd for LPGA players, despite your stronger 27.5° loft.
- **You're adding loft at impact.** 22° launch with a 27.5° 7-iron; Tour players launch at about half their loft. That fits a flip or early extension. The link to your S-posture is a coaching inference from your setup, not something the sim measures.
- **Your 7-iron and 8-iron went the same distance.** Only 2.3 yd apart, and the 8-iron had more ball speed (96 vs. 94 mph).
- **Probable misreads:** 7i_2 and 7i_5 show 7-iron spin of 1,388 and 1,694 rpm, and 7i_5, 8i_6, 9i_1 and PW_1 show smash of 1.40–1.45 (likely club-speed under-reads). Medians are used so they don't skew averages.

## Your handicap in context
- **For a 23–25 handicap, your distances are normal:** 8i/9i carries are at or above typical 25-handicap numbers, and the 7i is just below.
- **Your direction and contact are the gap:** the path and strike numbers, not distance, are what separate you from a mid-handicapper.
- **The A–F grades are measured against Tour standards,** so expect F/D grades for now. Realistic targets for the next few months: path −4° or better, face-to-path under +4°, iron smash 1.26+.
