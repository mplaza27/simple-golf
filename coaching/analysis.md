## One pattern runs through everything
- **Speed is good, contact isn't:** driver 99 mph and 7-iron ~81 mph club speed play like a 9–11 handicap; smash and carry play like a 22–27.
- **More effort, worse contact:** in Session 2 the 7-iron was swung 9 mph harder than Session 1 and produced less ball speed.
- **Long clubs come off the heel:** centring the strike late in Session 2 added 5 mph of driver ball speed and ~13 yd to the 3-hybrid and 7-wood.
- **Everything finishes right:** woods 36 yd right on average, irons 16 yd. Irons swing out-to-in (path −7.7°); the driver's path is almost neutral but its face is open (+3.7°).
