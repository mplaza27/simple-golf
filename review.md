## Priorities (most urgent first)
- **1. Setup and S-posture.** Get a neutral pelvis using the club-along-spine check (TPI). This drives both the over-the-top move and the early extension, and protects your lower back.
- **2. Club path from −9° to −3°, then to ±2°.** Headcover/gate drill with a slightly closed stance; start the downswing with the lower body.
- **3. Strike quality.** Iron median smash of 1.30 or better. Two-ball heel-gate drill plus foot-spray on the face.
- **4. Gapping and dispersion.** 10–12 yd between irons, 7-iron spread under ±6 yd.

## Drills
- **TPI wall (chair) drill:** keep your trail glute on the wall through impact. YouTube: `TPI early extension wall drill`, `TPI S-posture golf fix`.
- **Headcover path drill:** headcover 2 in. outside and 4–6 in. behind the ball. YouTube: `headcover drill over the top golf`.
- **Heel-gate two-ball drill:** a second ball 1 in. inside the target ball; miss it. YouTube: `stop hitting heel irons drill two ball`.

## Four-week targets
- **Week 1:** setup plus wall drill, half swings with the 8-iron.
- **Week 2:** add the gate drill. Target path −5° or better.
- **Week 3:** add the two-ball drill. Target 8-iron smash of 1.28 or better.
- **Week 4:** full-speed retest including 6i vs. 7W. Targets: path −3° or better, F2P under +3°, 7i–8i gap of 8 yd or more.

## On the course (DECADE)
- Until the path fix holds, aim left and let the fade work back. Play the shot shape you have.
